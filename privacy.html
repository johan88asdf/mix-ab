Privacy policy

MIXAOZX · version 1.0.1 · effective 25 November 2026

The short version: MIXAOZX has no account system, no analytics and no advertising SDK. Everything you see is rendered on your device, and your settings are stored only on your device.

1. What we collect

Nothing that leaves your device. The app does not create an account, does not request your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, and does not contain any third-party analytics, crash-reporting or advertising component.

2. What is stored on your device

  • Watchlist (coins you starred)
  • Language and appearance preference
  • The last help article you opened, so the app can reopen it

These values live in the app's local storage. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data from system settings, removes them permanently. We cannot read or recover them.

3. Network access

The app requests the INTERNET permission for one reason: so you can open an external web page in your browser when you tap "Open in browser". The built-in Help center and this policy are bundled inside the app and work offline. No content, telemetry or identifier is uploaded by the app itself.

4. Third parties

There are none. No SDK receives data from the app. If you voluntarily open an external link in your browser, that site's own policy applies from that point on.

5. Children

The app is a general-audience data-reference tool. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, of any age.

6. Data use and disclosure

Because no data is collected, none is sold, shared, transferred or used for profiling, marketing or advertising.

7. Your choices

You can erase everything the app stores at any time: clear the app's data in system settings, or uninstall the app. Both actions are immediate and complete.

8. Financial disclaimer

MIXAOZX displays static sample market values bundled with the app. It is not connected to any exchange feed, does not execute trades, does not hold assets, and provides no investment advice or recommendation.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text ships with the app and the effective date above is revised. Material changes will also be noted on the store listing.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: use the developer contact address shown on the app's store listing.
